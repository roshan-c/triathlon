# Project administration is reserved for one transferable owner

Only Instance Admins may create projects, and the creating admin becomes the project's initial owner. Each project has exactly one transferable owner who may rename or soft-delete it, configure its board, and issue invitations; ownership may transfer only to an existing member, after which the former owner becomes an ordinary member. Project members may manage tickets and sprints and create project-scoped access keys, but may not administer the project. Removing a member atomically unassigns their open tickets and disables their project access without rewriting historical attribution. The immutable creator and current owner are recorded separately, and an Instance Admin may recover project ownership. The unique Instance Owner role may transfer only to an existing Instance Admin; the former owner remains an admin, audit visibility moves to the new owner, and the current owner cannot be suspended or demoted.
